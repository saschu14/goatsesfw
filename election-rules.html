Weekly Guild Election

The sacred laws governing the transfer of power in our beloved democracy.

I. The Weekly Election

  1. A guild leader election shall be held once every week in the guild house.
  2. All guild members present at the election earns the right to vote.
  3. The sitting guild leader is eligible to run for re-election, even if they are unable to officially convene, but forfeit their right to vote.
  4. All guild members present are eligible guild leaders (except apprentices/lærlinger).
  5. The winner becomes guild leader until the next election.
  6. A leader may only remain in office for four (4) consecutive election cycles. However, previous leaders are eligible to run again after 2 election cycles of hiatus.

II. The Wheel of Democracy

  1. Guild speaker (defaults to the current guild leader) controls the pace of the election as well as the wheel of voting.
  2. The wheel of voting is spun once for each participating member.
  3. The result determines their voting power.
  4. Each spin is final unless a technical error occurs.

III. Casting Votes

  1. All members spin before voting begins.
  2. Each member casts their allocated votes for a candidate.
  3. The candidate with the highest total wins.

IV. Ties and Disputes

  1. A tie for first place triggers a runoff between tied candidates.
  2. Candidates duel for the honor of leading the guild, with the sitting leader deciding the method of combat.
  3. Disputes must be settled before results become official.

V. Transfer of Power

  1. The winner becomes the new guild leader.
  2. The outgoing leader transfers leadership after confirmation.
  3. The leader serves until the next weekly election.
