Guild Rules

Read these before joining the guild.

1. General Rules

  1. Respect other guild members.
  2. Don't intentionally cause problems for other members.
  3. Keep disagreements civil.

2. Guild Activity

  • Participate when you're able to.
  • Let someone know if you're going to be inactive for a long period.
  • Help other guild members when possible.

3. Events

  • Follow the event leader's instructions during organized activities.
  • If you sign up for something, try to show up.
  • Let the group know if your plans change.
Important: Rules may be updated as the guild develops.

4. Consequences

Breaking guild rules may result in a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from the guild depending on the situation.
